All numbers are computed in Python by `analysis/results_2026_10_22.py` and `analysis/business_case.py`
(tables in `analysis/results/2026-10-22_analysis/`). "Estimate" or "example" is said where it applies.

Words used:
- **Bound**: the 95 % range around the predicted weight; about 19 of 20 true weights should fall inside it.
- **Fixed scale**: the program measures mm per pixel from the two printed 50 mm markers and resizes every photo
  to 0.110 mm per pixel before counting.
- **Gauge R&R** (repeatability and reproducibility): one untouched pile photographed many times by 2 persons,
  2 phones, 2 heights, to see how much the photo alone changes the count.
- **95 % CI** (confidence interval): the range in which the true value lies with 95 % certainty, given our data.
- **p** (p-value): above 0.05 means the difference could easily be chance.
- **Bias**: average error with its sign; **mean absolute error**: average error without its sign.
- **In-sample**: tested on the same piles the model was made from (so not a real test).
- **Leave-one-pile-out**: make the model without one pile, test on that pile, repeat for every pile.
- **Points**: percentage points; from +-9.4 % to +-9.2 % is 0.2 points.
- **TARE**: the scale button that sets the display to 0.
- **t**: a factor from the t table (about 2.1 for 17 piles) that makes the bound wider when there are few piles.

## 4. The end user and the traffic light (deliverable 3): PLAN ONLY, not built

The user needs: the printed marker sheet (printed once at 100 %), white paper or a white table, a phone, and
the app link. Steps: put the markers on the white paper; pour the seeds and spread them so none touch; take one
photo from any height with both markers and all seeds in it; upload it in the app; read for example
"17.3 g (15.8 to 18.8 g)". No ruler, no measured height, no settings.

How long one measurement takes for the user is not measured yet (spreading 650 seeds is probably the slowest
part): we time it at the rehearsal on Sunday.

**Traffic light**, next to every result:
- **Green**: all checks pass. Show the weight and the bound.
- **Orange**: show the weight and the bound, plus one sentence on how to make a better photo.
- **Red**: no weight. One sentence on what to do.

Proposed checks. Each limit comes from our own photos. Rule: none of our 53 normal photos may turn orange or red, and the 6 others must give the expected light (IMG_2261 red; IMG_4183 and the 4 touching photos orange).

| Check | Light | Sentence for the user | What we compute | Limit and where it comes from |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| No marker | Red | "No marker found: put both markers fully in the photo." | Markers found | Already in the app (IMG_2261: -12.6 % without it) |
| Tilted phone | Orange | "Phone tilted: hold it flat above the seeds." | Size difference between the two markers (only when both are found) | Above 5 % (already a warning in the app). 47 of the 48 photos where both markers are found are at 3.7 % or less; IMG_4183 at 9.5 % was still only -2.2 %, so orange, not red |
| Seeds touching | Orange | "Seeds too close together: spread them so none touch." | Share of seed boxes that overlap another box by more than 20 % of the smaller box | Above 25 %: our normal photos are at 17 % or less, our touching photos at 42 to 51 %. No photo lies between 17 and 42 %, so the exact limit inside that gap is a choice |
| Too many seeds | Orange | "More seeds than we tested: split the sample in two photos." | Seed count | Above 651, the largest count we tested (P13, 34 g) |
| Other objects | Orange | "Something else is in the photo: remove it and take the photo again." | Dark shapes much larger than a seed that are not seeds or markers | Proposed: limit to set from our photos with a ruler and writing (17 Oct) against our clean photos |
| Shadow | Orange | "Strong shadow on the seeds: move so you do not block the light." | Brightness difference of the white background inside the seed area | Proposed: orange only above our darkest tested photo (P10), because our shadows (P10, P11, P13, IMG_4179) did not change the result |
| Blur | Orange | "Photo not sharp: hold the phone still and take it again." | Sharpness of the seed edges | Proposed: blur our own photos on purpose in steps; the limit is where the count drops by 2 % |

Not a check: only one marker found. It happens in 10 of our good photos: the 9 with a weight are all inside the
bounds and the 10th (IMG_2263, Gauge R&R) is +0.9 % from the average, so it would only give false orange lights.

Effort (estimate): the first four checks use numbers the app already has, about half a day with tests. Other
objects, shadow and blur need the extra measurement first, about one more day. Risk: a false orange during the
live demo; that is why every limit is tested on all 59 photos first.

## 5. Business case (deliverable 4)

**Time and cost** (measured from the photo times: first photo of one pile to the first photo of the next,
including scooping, weighing twice, spreading, the label photo, 2 photos and short breaks):

| Who and what | Minutes per pile | Cost per pile (example rate) |
|---|---|---|
| Hadi P01-P08, 10-24 g | 12.7 | 10.20 euro |
| Jia task 4, 11-23 g | 10.8 | 8.68 euro |
| Jia P09-P13, 27-34 g | 21.6 | 17.35 euro |
| One more photo of a pile that is already spread (Gauge R&R) | about 0.3 (19 s median) | 0.25 euro |

Example rate: 48.2 euro per hour (Eurostat, hourly labour cost 2024, Belgium, enterprises with 10 or more
employees). All our photo sessions together: 4.3 hours from the first to the last photo of each session
(`business_case.json`, without travel, preparation and the last pile of each session).

**What one more pile buys** (bound = t x s x sqrt(1 + 1/n), s = 3.97 %, our pooled spread; 12.7 min per pile):

| Piles | Bound | Gain of the next pile | Hours | Cost (example) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | +-14.1 % | 2.05 points | 0.8 | 41 euro |
| 6 | +-11.0 % | 0.64 points | 1.3 | 61 euro |
| 8 | +-10.0 % | 0.31 points | 1.7 | 82 euro |
| 10 | +-9.4 % | 0.18 points | 2.1 | 102 euro |
| 12 | +-9.1 % | 0.12 points | 2.5 | 122 euro |
| 15 | +-8.8 % | 0.07 points | 3.2 | 153 euro |
| 17 (now) | +-8.7 % | 0.06 points | 3.6 | 173 euro |
| 30 | +-8.3 % | 0.02 points | 6.3 | 306 euro |
| 100 | +-7.9 % | 0.00 points | 21.2 | 1,020 euro |

- **Floor**: never below +-7.8 % (1.96 x 3.97 %), however many piles. Part of the bound is our reference, not
  the photo: without the 1 g scale step it would be about +-7.8 % at 17 piles and about +-7.0 % as the floor
  (estimates). So the bound is on the safe side.
- **Stop rule**: stop when one more pile buys less than 0.2 points of bound. That is at **10 piles** (+-9.4 %,
  about 2 hours, about 100 euro). Why 0.2: below it, each 0.1 point costs 5.67 euro or more (one pile, 10.20
  euro, for 0.18 points), and the price only rises. If CNH needs a fixed accuracy instead, read the number of
  piles from the table (for example +-9 % needs 13 piles).
- **What more piles still give**: not a smaller bound, but a better known bound (with 17 piles the true spread
  could be 0.74 to 1.52 times 3.97 %, so the bound could really be +-6.4 to +-13.2 %) and more conditions
  covered (other days, persons, seed lots). That is why we kept Jia's piles after 10.
- **More photos of the same pile do not help much**: a photo costs 0.25 euro but only removes photo noise
  (1.3 %), not the pile-to-pile spread (3.97 %). Two photos per pile is enough.
- **Where the money should go instead**: a 0.01 g scale (removes the 1.73 % scale part) and a new calibration
  for each new seed lot (about 10 piles, about 100 euro; this is the cost that comes back every time).

## 4. The end user and the traffic light (deliverable 3): PLAN ONLY, not built

The user does 5 steps: put the two markers on a white table; pour and spread the seeds (none touching);
take one photo from any height with both markers and all seeds in it; upload it; read "17.3 g (15.6 to 19.0 g)".
No ruler, no measured height, no settings.

**Traffic light**, next to every result:
- **Green**: all checks pass. Show the weight and the bound.
- **Orange**: show the weight and the bound, plus one sentence on how to make a better photo.
- **Red**: no weight. One sentence on what to do.

Proposed checks. Each limit comes from our own photos; "proposed" ones need a small measurement first.

| Check | Light | Sentence for the user | What we compute | Limit and where it comes from |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| No marker | Red | "No marker found: put both markers fully in the photo." | Markers found by the program | Already in the app (IMG_2261: -12.6 % without it) |
| Only one marker | Orange | "Only one marker found: put both markers in the photo." | Number of markers | 2 in all our good photos |
| Tilted phone | Orange | "Phone tilted: hold it flat above the seeds." | Size difference between the two markers | Above 5 % (already a warning in the app). 58 of our 59 photos are at 3.7 % or less; IMG_4183 at 9.5 % was still only -2.2 %, so orange, not red |
| Seeds touching | Orange | "Seeds too close together: spread them so none touch." | Share of seed boxes that overlap another box by more than 20 % | Above 25 %: our normal photos are at 17 % or less, our touching photos at 42 to 51 % |
| Too many seeds | Orange | "More seeds than we tested: split the sample in two photos." | Seed count | Above 651, the largest count we tested (P13, 34 g) |
| Other objects | Orange | "Something else is in the photo: remove it and take the photo again." | Dark shapes much larger than a seed that are not seeds or markers | Proposed: limit to set from our photos with a ruler, note and writing (17 Oct pilot) against our clean photos |
| Shadow | Orange | "Strong shadow on the seeds: move so you do not block the light." | Brightness difference of the white background inside the seed area | Proposed: orange only above our darkest tested photo (P10), because our shadows (P10, P11, P13, IMG_4179) did not change the result |
| Blur | Orange | "Photo not sharp: hold the phone still and take it again." | Sharpness of the seed edges | Proposed: blur our own photos on purpose in steps and set the limit where the count drops by 2 % |

What it costs to build (estimate): the first five checks use numbers the app already has; about half a day of
work with tests. Shadow, other objects and blur need the extra measurement first; about one more day. Risk:
every new check can give a false orange during the live demo, so each limit is tested on all 59 of our photos
before it goes in (none of our good photos may turn orange).

## 5. Business case (deliverable 4)

**Time and cost** (measured from the photo times: first photo of one pile to the first photo of the next,
including spreading, the label photo, 2 photos, weighing and short breaks):

| Who and what | Minutes per pile | Cost per pile (example rate) |
|---|---|---|
| Hadi P01-P08, 10-24 g | 12.7 | 10.20 euro |
| Jia task 4, 11-23 g | 10.8 | 8.68 euro |
| Jia P09-P13, 27-34 g | 21.6 | 17.35 euro |
| One more photo of a pile that is already spread (Gauge R&R) | about 0.3 (19 s median) | 0.25 euro |

Example rate: 48.2 euro per hour (Eurostat, hourly labour cost 2024, Belgium, enterprises with 10 or more
employees). All our photo sessions together took about 4.3 hours of photo time (estimate from the first and
last photo of each session, without travel and preparation).

**What one more pile buys** (bound = t x s x sqrt(1 + 1/n), s = 3.97 %, our pooled spread; 12.7 min per pile):

| Piles | Bound | Gain of the next pile | Hours | Cost (example) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | +-14.1 % | 2.05 points | 0.8 | 41 euro |
| 6 | +-11.0 % | 0.64 points | 1.3 | 61 euro |
| 8 | +-10.0 % | 0.31 points | 1.7 | 82 euro |
| 10 | +-9.4 % | 0.18 points | 2.1 | 102 euro |
| 12 | +-9.1 % | 0.12 points | 2.5 | 122 euro |
| 15 | +-8.8 % | 0.07 points | 3.2 | 153 euro |
| 17 (now) | +-8.7 % | 0.06 points | 3.6 | 173 euro |
| 30 | +-8.3 % | 0.02 points | 6.3 | 306 euro |
| 100 | +-7.9 % | 0.00 points | 21.2 | 1,020 euro |

- **Floor**: never below +-7.8 % (1.96 x 3.97 %), however many piles. The floor belongs to our setup (1 g
  kitchen scale, one seed lot); without the scale-step error it would be about +-7.0 % (estimate).
- **Stop rule**: stop when one more pile improves the bound by less than 0.2 percentage points. That is at
  **10 piles** (+-9.4 %, about 2 hours, about 100 euro). There, one more pile costs 10.20 euro for 0.18 points,
  about 5.70 euro per 0.1 point, and it only gets more expensive.
- **More photos of the same pile do not help much**: a photo costs 0.25 euro but only removes photo noise
  (1.3 %), not the pile-to-pile spread (3.97 %). Two photos per pile is enough.
- **Where the money should go instead**: the scale (1.73 % of the spread; a 0.01 g scale would remove it) and
  the seed lot (calibrate again with about 10 piles, about 100 euro, when the lot or variety changes).
- **Caveat**: with 17 piles the true spread could be 0.74 to 1.52 times 3.97 % (95 %), so the bound at 17
  piles could really be between +-6.4 % and +-13.2 %.
